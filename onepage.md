# Metayb

Metayb is a digital ordering and stock-control platform for consumer-goods manufacturers and their distributors. It replaces manual call-based ordering, spreadsheet tracking, and disconnected warehouse updates with a single operating workflow.

Distributors can browse products, choose quantities, and place orders with clear pricing, discounts, and loyalty rewards. The system checks stock availability before reserving products, applies credit rules, and routes orders into approval or confirmation states based on available credit and business rules.

Manufacturing teams use the same system to track open orders, review status changes, and see warehouse impact in real time. The platform also calculates loyalty tiers and points automatically, helping drive repeat distributor purchases and retention.

For non-technical audiences, Metayb makes the process simpler: fewer errors, better stock visibility, faster approvals, and stronger distributor relationship management. It replaces fragmented manual processes with one consistent workflow for ordering, stock management, and loyalty rewards.
